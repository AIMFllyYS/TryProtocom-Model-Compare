Modelling and physics: one shared state (distance, wheel angle, crank angle) drives both 2.5D and 3D, so switching modes keeps the animation going. Wheel angle is distance divided by R, and crank angle is wheel angle times 16/48, giving the 3:1 ratio. Pedals stay horizontal. Each leg is a two-segment IK chain with the hip fixed and the sole on the pedal (footToPedal ≈ 0.012 m). The chain path comes from exact tangents and arcs around the two sprockets. The 2.5D view is layered SVG with independently animated parts. The 3D view is procedural Three.js geometry with a shadow-casting light. Audio is synthesized with Web Audio and starts on the first click.

Verified in the sandbox:

Wheel-to-crank ratio is 3.00 (48/16).
Everything stops at speed 0.
Wheel angle stays continuous across a mode switch.
No horizontal scroll at 390 px width.
No JS errors in the console; the only messages are GPU driver performance warnings.

Speed changes take effect instantly, with no acceleration.
